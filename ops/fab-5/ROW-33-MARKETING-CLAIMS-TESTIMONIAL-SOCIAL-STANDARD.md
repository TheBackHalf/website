## 2. How to classify a claim

| Class | Use when | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PERMITTED** | Aspiration, philosophy, or a fact that matches current product reality | May publish |
| **CONDITIONAL / REQUIRES SUPPORT** | Feature, price, date, availability, inclusion, AI identity, testimonial, endorsement | Publish only with substantiation, permission, or required disclosure |
| **PROHIBITED** | Unsupported transformation, health treatment, financial guarantees, fake social proof, refunds, 24/7 support, live community on August 31 | Do not publish |

When classification is unclear: **FOUNDER/LEGAL JUDGMENT REQUIRED**. Do not invent a resolution.

## 3. Permissible launch claims

### A. Brand / philosophy claims — PERMITTED

Possibility, intention, aliveness, reflection, life design, personal choice, exploration, becoming, purpose, adventure, contribution, relationships.

Health and career may appear as **life-design domains for reflection**, not as treatment or income promises.

Aspirational brand language may be used when it does **not** guarantee a specific measurable result.

### B. Factual product claims — CONDITIONAL

A factual claim may be used only when it accurately reflects **current** product reality or authoritative approved documentation:

- What The Back Half Blueprint contains
- Number of Journey chapters/stages (seven)
- Available downloads
- Lumina functionality (AI Guide)
- AI Founder / AI Kimberly functionality (no public AI Kimberly chat at launch)
- Support channels (form + `support@thebackhalf.org`; typical 3-day / 72-hour goal)
- Membership / community **offer** language (not live-community-on-August-31)
- Language availability
- Pricing
- Features
- Access periods
- Launch dates (**August 31, 2026**)

If the product does not do it yet, do not say it does.

### C. Experience claims — PERMITTED when accurate

“designed to help,” “invites you to,” “gives you a framework,” “provides tools for,” “helps you explore,” “creates space to,” “guides reflection.”

Do not convert these into guaranteed outcome statements.

---

## 4. Prohibited / unsubstantiated transformation claims — PROHIBITED

Do not claim, imply, or hashtag that participation will:

- Cure a medical condition
- Treat mental illness
- Replace therapy
- Eliminate depression or anxiety
- Heal trauma
- Produce guaranteed weight loss or health outcomes
- Repair a marriage or relationship
- Guarantee career advancement, employment, business success, income, investment returns, or wealth
- Guarantee happiness, fulfillment, a “magical life,” transformation, or any specific measurable personal outcome

Also prohibit unsupported: “clinically proven,” “scientifically proven,” “guaranteed,” “risk free,” “works for everyone,” “will change your life,” or material equivalents.

---

## 5. Health / wellness

Health is an approved Journey domain. Ordinary wellness and life-design language may be used.

Do not represent The Back Half, Lumina, AI Kimberly, the Blueprint, or the Journey as providing medical diagnosis or treatment, mental-health diagnosis, therapy, psychiatric care, clinical treatment, emergency intervention, or professional medical advice.

---

## 6. Financial + career

May discuss career intention, purpose, professional reflection, life design, goals, and possibilities.

Must not promise income, investment returns, employment, promotion, business revenue, financial independence, or wealth creation unless specifically substantiated and Founder-approved.

---

## 7. Testimonials

Any testimonial must be authentic; from a real person unless explicitly identified otherwise; used with permission; accurately represented; not materially edited to change meaning; not fabricated; not generated and presented as a real customer; not misleading; and not used to imply guaranteed results.

If a result is exceptional, do not imply it is typical without approved context.

**Launch rule:** Do not invent testimonials. Do not create placeholder customer praise. The August 28–31 campaign contains **none**.

---

## 8. Testimonial permission + recordkeeping

Before publishing a testimonial, retain internally (do not post this register publicly):

- Person/source
- Permission/authorization
- Approved quotation or content
- Date permission obtained
- Where it may be used
- Any material relationship
- Any compensation, free product, or incentive
- Required disclosure
- Approval status

Store records with other restricted operations files. Do not expose personal data on the Founder review page.

---

## 9. Endorsements + material connections

If the speaker is paid, given free access or gifts, compensated as an affiliate, employed, contracted, an owner, or otherwise incentivized, disclose the relationship clearly. Do not bury the disclosure.

Official company replies follow this standard and Row 83. Do not impersonate Kimberly on social replies. Public voice is **The Back Half**.

---

## 10. AI-generated testimonials — PROHIBITED

Prohibited: AI-generated fake customers; AI quotations presented as genuine; synthetic people presented as Architects; AI transformation stories represented as real experiences.

AI may assist editing a **real** approved testimonial only if the final representation remains truthful and does not materially alter meaning.

## 11. AI Founder — Kimberly M. Walker (AI)

The company may use the approved AI Founder / digital twin.

Marketing must not deceptively imply that an AI-generated Founder interaction or video is a live human interaction when it is not.

Use **Kimberly M. Walker (AI)** where the surface **is** the AI Founder representation.

Do not rename the Founder. Do not remove the AI Founder. Do not weaken the approved Founder experience.

The website Founder section (“Hi, I’m Kimberly,” Founder photography) is the **human Founder** surface unless a specific asset is the AI Founder.

Lumina is the participant-facing **AI Guide**, not the Founder. Identify Lumina as AI.

**Compliant:** “Lumina — your AI Guide” plus quiet first-comment link to `https://thebackhalf.org/legal/ai-disclosure` when Lumina appears.  
**Noncompliant:** An AI Founder video or chat presented as a live conversation with Kimberly without identification.

---

## 12. Other AI / synthetic media

Decorative or AI-assisted production does **not** require disclosure merely because tools assisted.

Disclose when omission could materially mislead about who is speaking, whether a person is real, whether a testimonial is real, whether an experience occurred, or whether an endorsement is authentic.
